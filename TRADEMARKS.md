# Names and logos

The source code is MIT-licensed, and you may fork it, change it and run it, including as a
paid or free service. The MIT licence covers the **code**. It does not grant rights in the
project's **names and logos**:

- "LeilMat", "Leil" and "Leil AI"
- the INFINITE name and logo, and the project's own logo and brand artwork (`apps/web/public`,
  `docs/assets`)

**You may**

- say your work is "based on LeilMat" or "a fork of LeilMat", and link to this repository;
- redistribute the code unmodified, with the licence and `NOTICE.md` intact;
- use the names descriptively and truthfully, e.g. in a citation or a compatibility note.

**Please don't**

- name a fork, product or service "LeilMat" or "Leil" anything, or use the logo, in a way that
  suggests it is the official project or endorsed by it;
- present results from your own instance as results from the free online version.

If you are unsure, open an issue and ask. This note describes how the project asks its names
to be used; it is not a registration and not legal advice.
